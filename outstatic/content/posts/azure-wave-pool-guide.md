If you're staying at [Azure Urban Resort Residences](https://www.azure.com.ph/) in Parañaque, the wave pool is one of the biggest draws of the whole property, a man-made beach and wave pool inside a residential complex. But access isn't automatic just because you're staying in one of the towers. It runs on its own schedule, has its own dress code, and needs a visitor's pass to get in. Here's everything you need to know before you go, from [Azure Staycation by Siaos](https://azurestaycationbysiaos.com).

## Quick Answers

- **Wave pool location:** Azure Urban Resort Residences, Km. 16 West Service Road, Bicutan, Parañaque City
- **Wave pool days:** Wednesdays to Mondays (closed Tuesdays)
- **Wave pool hours:** 7:00 AM to 12:00 NN, and 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM
- **Price:** ₱250 per head, per shift, cash only
- **Wave shifts:** 8:30, 9:30, 10:30, 11:30 (morning) and 2:30, 3:30, 4:30, 5:30 (afternoon)

## Pool Registration: Important Reminders

![Pool registration location](/images/blog/azure-wave-pool-guide/pool-registration-location.webp)

1. **Present your visitor's pass before queuing at the Registration Booth.** A lost visitor pass is charged a ₱500 penalty.
2. **Only one representative should fall in line** to avoid a long queue for your whole group.
3. **PWD or Senior Citizen discount** requires a valid ID. No ID, no discount.
4. **Guests 3 ft. tall and below enter free.**
5. **Pay ₱250.00 per shift at the Pool Cashier.** Cash only, and please pay the exact amount for a faster transaction.
6. **Payment is non-refundable.**
7. **Double-check your swim band, visitor pass, receipt, and change** before you leave the cashier.
8. **Ask the security guard for assistance** putting on your swim band if you need it.
9. **Wet clothes are not allowed inside the tower.**
10. **Wear proper swimwear** and always follow pool rules and regulations.
11. **No smoking or vaping.**
12. **Don't leave your child unattended.**

## Wave Pool Dress Code

![Proper and improper swimwear attire chart for Azure wave pool](/images/blog/azure-wave-pool-guide/azure-pool-dress-code.webp)

Azure enforces a strict swimwear policy. Sports apparel not designed for swimming is not allowed.

**Proper attire:**
- One-piece or two-piece bathing suits
- Swim trunks, swim/surf shirts
- Swim briefs (long or short)
- Religious swimwear (modest swim styles)
- Swimmers' diapers for young kids
- T-shirts, flip-flops, and sarongs (deck wear only, not for swimming)

**Not allowed:**
- Cut-off shorts, denim shorts
- Halter tops, leotards, sports bras
- Thongs, tennis dresses
- Cotton underwear, tube socks
- Basketball shorts, boxers, compression shirts or shorts
- Regular (non-swim) diapers
- Aqua socks

If you show up in the wrong attire, you may be turned away at the pool, so pack proper swimwear before your stay.

## Wave Pool Rules and Guidelines

![Wave pool and kiddie pool guidelines covering showers, footbaths, and belongings](/images/blog/azure-wave-pool-guide/pool-guidelines.webp)

- Shower before entering the pool and after using the toilet facilities
- Wear proper swimwear and your color-coded swim band at all times in the pool area
- Use the footbath when entering and exiting
- Bring only essentials in a beach bag, including slippers. Plastic bags are not allowed
- Place your beach bag in the racks provided
- Always carry your visitor's pass. It doubles as your gate, pool, and lobby access. Lost or damaged passes cost ₱500
- Only plastic bottled water or a personal tumbler is allowed inside the pool area. No breakable bottles or tin cans
- Non-toilet-trained children must wear swim diapers
- Follow lifeguard and pool attendant instructions, and report any accident or injury immediately
- Do not leave minors unsupervised. Management is not responsible for incidents involving unattended children

## How to Get to the Wave Pool from Santorini Tower

1. Go down to the Ground Floor
2. Facing the glass door on your way out of the tower, turn left
3. Walk straight to the corner, just before the ramp to Rio Parking
4. Turn left again
5. Follow the pathway on your right
6. You'll see the tent where wristbands are sold for pool access

If you're staying in a different tower, the layout is similar, ask your host or the lobby concierge for tower-specific directions.

## Before You Head Back to Your Unit

Head down to the wave pool in casual clothes, then change into your swimwear once you're there, or wear it underneath your clothes. On the way back, you must be completely dry before re-entering the tower.

- Make sure you're completely dry before re-entering the tower. Wet clothes aren't allowed inside, and Azure charges a ₱2,000 penalty for entering wet
- Wear slippers on your way back in
- Keep your visitor's pass safe. It's ₱500 to replace if lost, and the wave pool area is public, so watch your belongings too

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Azure wave pool open every day?

No. It's open Wednesdays through Mondays, and closed on Tuesdays.

### How much does the wave pool cost per person?

₱250 per head, per shift, cash only.

### Do I need a visitor's pass to use the wave pool?

Yes. You need a visitor's pass to purchase swim bands, and without one, you can't access the pool.

### How many visitor passes come with my unit?

It depends on unit size: 4 passes for a 1-bedroom, 6 for a 2-bedroom, and 8 for a 3-bedroom.

### What happens if I lose my visitor's pass?

You'll be charged ₱500 for a lost or damaged visitor's pass.

### Can I bring my own drinks to the pool?

Only plastic bottled water or a personal tumbler is allowed. Glass bottles and tin cans are not permitted.

### Is there a discount for PWD or senior citizen guests?

Yes, but a valid ID must be presented at registration. No ID means no discount.

### Do young children pay to enter the wave pool?

Guests 3 ft. tall and below enter free.
**Please note: The height requirement is strictly enforced by the pool administration.**
