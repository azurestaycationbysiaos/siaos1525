One of the most common questions first-time guests ask before booking a staycation at [Azure Urban Resort Residences](/) is whether every unit follows the same house rules.

The short answer is **yes and no**.

Every staycation host has their own house rules designed to protect their property and provide a safe, comfortable, and enjoyable experience for future guests. These rules may vary depending on the unit and can include policies on:

* Check-in and check-out procedures
* Cooking and kitchen use
* Room decorations
* Visitor access
* Maximum occupancy
* Proper use of unit amenities
* Cleaning and housekeeping expectations

However, all guests—regardless of which unit they book—must also comply with the official policies enforced by Azure Urban Resort Residences. These community-wide regulations apply to every resident, visitor, and staycation guest within the property.

## Azure Policies That Every Staycation Guest Must Follow

Azure Urban Resort Residences has property-wide policies to help maintain a safe, clean, and enjoyable environment for everyone. These include rules related to:

* Noise disturbances and quiet hours
* Smoking only in designated areas
* Proper disposal of cigarette butts and trash
* Illegal parking
* Overcrowding and unregistered guests
* Unauthorized entry into the property
* Hanging clothes from balconies
* Wearing wet clothing in common areas
* Pet policies
* Vandalism or damage to common areas
* Fire safety and building regulations

Violating these community policies may result in penalties imposed directly by Azure's property management, regardless of which host you booked with. For this reason, guests should always familiarize themselves with both their host's house rules and Azure's official property policies before arriving.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Azure's policies the same as my host's house rules?

Not exactly.

Each staycation host creates house rules that apply specifically to their unit. These may cover topics such as check-in procedures, cooking guidelines, visitor policies, decorations, or the proper use of unit amenities.

In addition, Azure Urban Resort Residences has its own property-wide policies that every guest must follow, regardless of which host they book with. These regulations cover areas such as noise, smoking, parking, overcrowding, fire safety, and the responsible use of shared facilities. They are enforced by Azure's property management and help ensure a safe and enjoyable environment for all residents and guests.
