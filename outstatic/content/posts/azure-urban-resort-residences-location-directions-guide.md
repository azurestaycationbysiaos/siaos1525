Azure Urban Resort Residences is a beach-themed condominium complex in Bicutan, Parañaque City, best known for bringing the Philippines' first large-scale man-made beach and wave pool into a residential development. If you're searching for its exact location, how to get there, what's around it, or how to book a staycation unit, this guide covers everything in one place.

## What Is Azure Urban Resort Residences?

Azure Urban Resort Residences is a multi-tower condo development designed by the architectural firm Broadway Malyan. Each tower is named after a famous beach destination, including Boracay, Rio, St. Tropez, Miami, Bahamas, and Santorini. The development is built around a man-made beach and wave pool, giving guests a beach-resort feel without leaving Metro Manila. Many unit owners rent out their condos as short-term staycation spaces, which is how Azure became one of the most searched staycation destinations in the metro.

## Where Is Azure Urban Resort Residences Located?

Azure Urban Resort Residences sits along the West Service Road in Bicutan, Parañaque City, Metro Manila. It's part of the Bicutan / Sucat corridor, right beside SM Bicutan and close to the Sucat exit of the South Luzon Expressway (SLEX). Its position between Parañaque and Taguig makes it an easy stop whether you're coming from the airport, Makati, BGC, or the south.

**General address reference:** Azure Urban Resort Residences, West Service Road, Bicutan, Parañaque City, Metro Manila. Each tower (Santorini, Rio, Bahamas, and others) has its own building entrance, so it's worth confirming the exact tower with your host before heading over.

## How to Get to Azure From NAIA

Azure is one of the closest staycation destinations to [Ninoy Aquino International Airport](https://www.airport-suppliers.com/airport/ninoy-aquino-international-airport/), roughly 4 to 5 kilometers away depending on which terminal you're coming from.

- **By taxi or ride-hailing app [(Grab)](https://www.grab.com/ph/), [(inDrive)](https://indrive.com/en-ph):** The most convenient option. The trip usually takes 10 to 20 minutes depending on traffic and terminal.
- **By private car:** Take the West Service Road heading toward Sucat/Bicutan. Azure is visible along the road, right next to SM Bicutan.
- **By public transport:** From the airport, you can take a jeepney or UV Express bound for Bicutan, then walk or tricycle the short remaining distance to the Azure compound.

Because of this proximity, Azure is a popular layover or pre-flight staycation option for travelers with early morning or late-night flights.

## Is Azure Urban Resort Residences in Parañaque?

Yes. [Azure Urban Resort Residences](https://www.azure.com.ph/) is officially located in Bicutan, Parañaque City. It sits close to the Parañaque-Taguig boundary, which is why some directories mistakenly list it under Taguig. If you're navigating by map app, search "Azure Urban Resort Residences, Parañaque" for the most accurate pin.

## Azure's Man-Made Beach and Amenities

The centerpiece of Azure is its man-made beach and wave pool, which produces beach-style waves at scheduled intervals throughout the day. Beyond the beach, the development includes a lagoon pool, an infinity lap pool, a kids' pool area, a basketball court, and clubhouse facilities with dining and lounge spaces. Some amenities, like the lap pool and clubhouse, are reserved for condo owners and long-term residents, while the beach and wave pool are generally accessible to staycation guests for a separate fee set by building management, not by individual unit owners.
