## Planning Your Stay at Azure Urban Resort Residences

One of the most common questions first-time guests ask is:

**"What amenities can I access during my stay?"**

[Azure Urban Resort Residences](https://www.azure.com.ph/amenities/) offers several resort-style amenities that staycation guests can enjoy during their visit. This guide highlights the guest-accessible facilities, along with helpful information about convenience stores, ATM locations, and cash services to help you prepare for a smooth and enjoyable stay.

The property is also conveniently located near [SM City Bicutan](https://www.smsupermalls.com/mall-directory/sm-city-bicutan/information), making it easy for guests to grab extra essentials or dine outside the property if needed.

## Azure Beach

![Azure Beach at Azure Urban Resort Residences](/images/blog/amenities/azure-beach-azure-urban-resort-residences.webp)

The Azure Beach is one of the property's most iconic attractions. With its fine white sand and resort-inspired atmosphere, it's the perfect place to relax, take photos, or simply unwind during your stay.

## Azure Wave Pool

![Azure Wave Pool at Azure Urban Resort Residences](/images/blog/amenities/azure-wave-pool-azure-urban-resort-residences.webp)

The Azure Wave Pool gives guests the experience of enjoying beach-like waves without leaving Metro Manila. It's one of the highlights of staying at Azure Urban Resort Residences.

*Ask your host for the current wave pool schedule before your stay.*

## Beach Bar

![Beach Bar at Azure Urban Resort Residences](/images/blog/amenities/azure-beach-bar-azure-urban-resort-residences.webp)

Located beside Azure Beach, the Beach Bar provides a relaxing space where guests can enjoy the resort ambiance while taking a break from swimming or exploring the property.

## Children's Beach Playground

![Children's Beach Playground at Azure Urban Resort Residences](/images/blog/amenities/childrens-beach-playground-azure-urban-resort-residences.webp)

Families traveling with children can enjoy the dedicated beach playground featuring water-friendly play structures designed for younger guests.

![Beach Volleyball Court at Azure Urban Resort Residences](/images/blog/amenities/beach-volleyball-azure-urban-resort-residences.webp)

Guests looking for outdoor activities can enjoy the beach volleyball area, which adds to the property's fun, tropical atmosphere.

## ATM Locations

Need cash during your stay?

Azure Urban Resort Residences has two convenient ATM locations:

- One ATM inside the Azure Clubhouse
- One ATM located between Positano Tower and Miami Tower

Although ATMs are available, we recommend bringing enough cash before arriving, especially during weekends, holidays, or peak travel periods.

## Cash Out & Cash-In Services

If you need additional cash or want to top up your e-wallet, the property offers convenient options.

### Cash Out

Available at:

- **7-Eleven Maldives Tower**

### Cash In

A **Pay & Go** machine is available at:

- **Lawson Boracay Tower**

## Convenience Stores

Need snacks, drinks, toiletries, or other daily essentials?

Guests can visit the following stores within Azure Urban Resort Residences:

- Stopover, Bahamas tower
- Lawsonm, Boracay tower
- Kerchin Convenience Store, Maldives tower
- 7-Eleven, Maldives tower
- Jonson Mart, Maui tower

These stores make it convenient to purchase everyday necessities without leaving the property.
