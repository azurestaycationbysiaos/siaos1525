Most men planning a proposal think about the ring, the words, maybe the venue. Very few think about **when**, down to the hour — and it's one of the biggest reasons a proposal setup looks incredible in person but falls flat in photos.

## Why timing matters as much as decor

Natural light changes fast in Metro Manila. A balcony that looks warm and golden at 5:30 PM can look flat and grey by 6:15. If your photographer or videographer is capturing the moment, the window for the best light is often only 20-30 minutes wide.

## What we actually plan around

For our proposal staycation setups at Azure Urban Resort Residences in Parañaque, we map timing based on:

- **Golden hour** — roughly 30-45 minutes before sunset, for warm, soft light that flatters both of you on camera
- **Indoor ambient light** — if you're proposing indoors, we time artificial lighting (candles, string lights) to kick in right as natural light fades, so there's no awkward transition
- **Guest and staff movement** — we schedule around building activity so there's no interruption right at the moment you kneel

## A simple rule of thumb

If you're planning your own proposal without a coordinator, aim for **30 minutes before official sunset** for outdoor or balcony proposals. For indoor setups, warm white lighting (avoid harsh overhead lights) makes an enormous difference in both mood and photos.
