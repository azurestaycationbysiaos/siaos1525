## Budget & Casual Food at SM City Bicutan

Familiar, affordable food chains for a quick and easy meal at SM City Bicutan.

- **KFC** — Fried chicken, chicken meals, sandwiches and group meals. [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=KFC+SM+City+Bicutan)

- **Reyes Barbecue** — Filipino barbecue and rice meals. [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Reyes+Barbecue+SM+City+Bicutan)
- **Razon's of Guagua** — Pancit palabok, halo-halo and other Filipino favorites. [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Razon%27s+of+Guagua+SM+City+Bicutan)
