[Azure Urban Resort Residences](https://www.azure.com.ph/location/) is located along SLEX West Service Road corner D. Soledad Avenue in Bicutan, Parañaque City, beside SM City Bicutan. For commuters, the easiest landmarks to remember are SM City Bicutan, the Bicutan Exit, SLEX West Service Road, and D. Soledad Avenue.

Azure Urban Resort Residences is in Bicutan, Parañaque City, beside SM City Bicutan and near the Bicutan SLEX Exit. The most practical public-transport route from Makati is usually MRT-3 to Ayala Station, transfer to One Ayala, then take a bus toward Bicutan. ## Quick Answer: What Is the Easiest Way to Get to Azure?

For most guests coming from Metro Manila, one of the easiest public-transport routes is MRT-3 to Ayala Station, walk to One Ayala, take a bus toward Bicutan, get off around Bicutan or SM City Bicutan, then walk toward Azure.

If you are coming from Alabang, look for buses or jeepneys going toward Bicutan. If you are arriving at NAIA with luggage, Grab or taxi is usually the simplest option.

**Important:** PNR Metro Manila commuter service is not currently a practical route to Azure. Avoid older online guides that still recommend taking PNR to Bicutan or Sucat.

## From Makati to Azure

- **Option 1:** MRT-3 to Ayala Station → One Ayala → bus toward Bicutan/Sucat/Alabang → get off at Bicutan/SM Bicutan → walk to Azure.
- **Option 2:** From the Pasay/Taft side, use a jeep or bus that serves Bicutan/FTI, and confirm with the driver that it passes Bicutan or SM Bicutan.
- **Tip:** If using One Ayala, confirm the destination sign and current loading point before boarding because routes and schedules can change.

## From Pasay to Azure

- Take a jeep or bus toward Bicutan/FTI where available, then get off around Bicutan or SM City Bicutan.
- Another practical option is to travel toward Ayala/One Ayala, then take the Bicutan bus.
- Ask: "Bicutan po, SM Bicutan?" before boarding.

## From Manila to Azure

- From Taft/Pasay, take a Bicutan-bound jeep or bus.
- From Ermita/Malate, a practical rail connection is LRT-1 to EDSA Station → MRT Taft → MRT Ayala → One Ayala → Bicutan bus.
- From Quiapo and nearby areas, check for a current Bicutan-bound UV Express; route availability can change, so verify the destination sign before boarding.

## From Quezon City to Azure

- Take MRT-3 southbound to Ayala Station → walk to One Ayala → take the Bicutan/Sucat/Alabang bus → get off at Bicutan/SM Bicutan → walk to Azure.

## From Alabang to Azure

- Take a bus or jeep toward Bicutan/Sucat and confirm that it passes Bicutan.
- Some West Service Road jeepneys can operate as cutting trips, meaning a transfer may be necessary. For guests with luggage, the bus may be easier.
- See this [commuter discussion on Alabang–Bicutan service-road routes](https://www.reddit.com/r/commutersph/comments/1uxyj6c/alabang_to_bicutan_west_service_road_jeep/) for first-hand rider notes.

## From Laguna to Azure

- From Biñan or Santa Rosa, take a bus toward Alabang or Ayala/One Ayala, then transfer to a Bicutan-bound bus.
- From Calamba and other parts of Laguna, travel toward Alabang or Ayala first, then continue to Bicutan.
- Allow extra time for SLEX traffic — treat any travel time as an estimate only.

## From Bulacan to Azure

- There is usually no single convenient direct public-transport route from most parts of Bulacan.
- One practical pattern is Bulacan → Manila/EDSA or Pasay → Ayala/One Ayala → Bicutan.
- Another is Bulacan → Pasay → Bicutan/FTI jeep or bus. For a sense of a comparable long-distance leg, see [Rome2Rio's Balagtas to Pasay route](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Balagtas/Pasay).
- With luggage or a group, a private vehicle or ride-hailing service may be more convenient.

## From NAIA Terminals to Azure

For travelers arriving with luggage, Grab or taxi is generally the simplest option from NAIA to Azure. Traffic around the airport can change travel time significantly, so avoid expecting an exact number of minutes.

### NAIA Terminal 2

### NAIA Terminal 3

This is one of the closer terminals to Azure by road. Grab/taxi is still the simplest option, especially after a flight.

Ask the driver: "Dadaan po ng Bicutan/SM Bicutan?" Confirm before boarding.

## Is PNR Available to Azure?

No. Do not rely on PNR as a current commuting option. Metro Manila PNR commuter operations were suspended in 2024 for North-South Commuter Railway construction. Older blogs that say "take PNR to Bicutan Station" are outdated.

For current rail-based travel, use LRT-1 and MRT-3 as connections to bus routes toward Bicutan.

## Ride-Hailing and Motorcycle Taxi Options to Azure

If you prefer not to take a jeepney, bus, UV Express or train connection, you can book a ride directly to Azure Urban Resort Residences. Availability and fares can vary by time, traffic, demand and pickup location, so check the app before booking.

### Grab

Grab is one of the most practical choices for Azure guests, particularly if you have luggage, are traveling as a couple or family, or are arriving from NAIA. Grab Philippines offers private-car and taxi booking, with upfront pricing for GrabCar and ride tracking through the app.

Official information: [Grab Philippines transport](https://www.grab.com/ph/transport/)

**Best for:** NAIA arrivals, families, couples, guests with luggage, and door-to-door trips.

**Tip:** Set the destination to Azure Urban Resort Residences and check that the pickup pin is correct before confirming.

### JoyRide

JoyRide offers both motorcycle taxi and four-wheel services. Its official service list includes MC Taxi, Car, Taxi, Super Taxi and Airport Transfer. JoyRide also operates an airport-transfer lounge at NAIA Terminal 3 near Gate 7.

Official information: [JoyRide PH](https://joyride.com.ph/)

**Best for:** Airport transfers, private car rides, or solo commuters using MC Taxi.

For NAIA Terminal 3, JoyRide's official site currently lists its Super Taxi Airport Transfer Lounge in the Arrivals Area near Gate 7.

### Move It

Move It is a motorcycle-taxi service designed for quick point-to-point trips through Metro Manila traffic. Passengers can enter their pickup and drop-off points in the app and choose cash or GCash payment.

Official information: [Move It – How It Works](https://moveit.com.ph/how-it-works/)

**Best for:** Solo guests without bulky luggage who want a motorcycle taxi to reach Azure faster through congested roads.

**Not ideal for:** airport travelers carrying suitcases, groups, or guests who need a car.

### Angkas

Angkas offers motorcycle-taxi passenger rides and currently lists Parañaque and other Metro Manila areas among its service locations. Its service is useful for solo travelers with light belongings.

Official information: [Angkas Passenger](https://www.angkas.com/our-services/passenger) &middot; Check current service areas: [Angkas Locations](https://www.angkas.com/about/locations)

**Best for:** Solo commuters, light bags, and trips where avoiding traffic is more important than having a car.

**Not ideal for:** large luggage, families, multiple passengers, or airport transfers with suitcases.

### inDrive

inDrive is a car-based ride-hailing option available in Metro Manila. Unlike platforms that rely mainly on a fixed upfront fare, inDrive lets passengers request a price and review offers from drivers. This can be useful when comparing the cost of a trip to Azure, especially for longer journeys.

Current availability: [inDrive Philippines app listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sinet.startup.inDriver)

**Best for:** Guests who want another car-booking option and are comfortable comparing driver offers.

**Tip:** Agree on the fare in the app before the trip and verify the driver's details and vehicle before getting in.

### Green GSM

Green GSM is an electric-vehicle ride-hailing service in the Philippines. Its official site describes Green GSM Car as an electric car service that can be booked through its app. The service is particularly relevant to guests who prefer a car ride and want an electric-vehicle option.

Official information: [Green GSM](https://www.greengsm.ph/en) &middot; [Green GSM Car details](https://www.greengsm.ph/en/greengsm-car)

**Best for:** Car passengers, couples, families and guests with luggage.

**Tip:** Availability depends on the number of Green GSM vehicles near your pickup point, so check the app before relying on it for a time-sensitive trip.

### Other Ride-Hailing Options

Availability changes over time, so it is better to treat additional services as alternatives rather than assuming a particular app will always have a driver. Guests can check their preferred ride-hailing apps before leaving and compare availability, vehicle type and estimated fare. Other ride-hailing services may also be available depending on your location and current driver supply.

### Motorcycle Taxi vs. Car: What Should Azure Guests Choose?

- Choose a car if you have luggage, are traveling with children, are traveling as a couple or group, or are coming directly from NAIA.
- Choose a motorcycle taxi if you are traveling alone, have only a small bag, and want to reduce the time spent in traffic.
- Do not book a motorcycle taxi if your luggage cannot be safely accommodated.
- For proposals, birthdays or celebrations where you are carrying decorations, gifts, flowers or equipment, use a car instead.

### Ride-Hailing Pickup Tip at Azure

When booking a car or motorcycle taxi, use the exact Azure Urban Resort Residences location and check the map pin. If the driver asks for a landmark, say: "Azure Urban Resort Residences, Bicutan, Parañaque, near SM City Bicutan and the Bicutan SLEX Exit."

If the app allows you to message the driver, send the tower/building information and your preferred meeting point once you have confirmed your booking. For a staycation, follow the host's check-in instructions rather than assuming the driver can enter the residential property.

### Ride-Hailing Safety Checklist

- Book through the official app instead of accepting an unsolicited ride.
- Check the driver's name, photo, plate number and vehicle details before entering.
- Use the in-app trip-sharing or safety features when available.
- Do not give your OTP or account verification code to a driver.
- If the pickup pin is wrong, correct it before the driver arrives.
- For motorcycle taxis, wear the required helmet and follow the operator's safety instructions.
